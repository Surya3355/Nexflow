Three ways to lay out the main screens

Login, Reports and Admin are the same in all three versions. The difference is how you find a case and work on it.

A · List + pages

Home is a list of cases. Tap a case and you go to its own page, and the customer has a separate page too. This is the most familiar layout and works best on a phone.

6 screens · Home, Customer, Case, Reports, Admin, Login

Open version A

B · Board

Cases are cards sitting in their stage's column, so you see the whole pipeline at once. A case opens in a side panel. Great on a laptop for managers; on a phone you scroll sideways through the columns.

6 screens · the Case page becomes mostly a side panel

Open version B

C · Split view

Works like an email inbox: customers on the left, everything about the selected customer on the right, with each case expandable. Fewest clicks on a laptop. On a phone it stacks, which feels more cramped.

4 screens · Home, Customer and Case become one

Open version C

You can also mix them, for example C as Home with B's board added as a chip ("List / Board").
